What's included

Everything in the Premium Wash, plus:

  • Seat removal
  • Deep sanitising of the seats

Interior

With the seats out, we can work the cabin far more deeply.

The seats are sanitised individually and the areas that are normally hard to reach get a thorough clean.

Exterior

On top of the full Premium Wash process, with its glass decontamination and premium sealant, we also carry out:

  • Paint decontamination
  • Deep wheel cleaning
  • Tyre cleaning

How long it takes

Around 8 hours on average for a car. SUVs, MPVs and large vans take longer, and the estimate is shown when you book. It may vary with the condition of the vehicle.

Who it is for

For vehicles that need a deep clean, and for customers who want the highest level of care and detail on their car.

It is especially suited to vehicles with built-up dirt, interiors that need deep sanitising, or cars that have not had a detailed treatment in a long time.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the Detailed Wash Interior + Exterior take?
Around 8 hours on average for a car. SUVs, MPVs and large vans take longer, and the estimate is shown when you book.
Are the seats really removed?
Yes. The seats are removed so the cleaning and sanitising can go deeper.
Does the Detailed Wash include paint decontamination?
Yes. Paint decontamination is included in this service.
How is the Premium different from the Detailed Wash?
The Detailed Wash adds a much higher level of work, including seat removal, deep sanitising, paint decontamination and deep cleaning of wheels and tyres.
Can I have only the interior or only the exterior done?
Yes. The Detailed Wash Interior or Exterior does the same work, only on the area you choose when booking.
